CERTIFIED STANDARDS

🇮🇳
ISI Mark
Bureau of Indian Standards certification. Mandatory for helmets sold in India. Ensures minimum safety standards.
🇺🇸
DOT
US Department of Transportation standard. Rigorous impact absorption, penetration resistance & retention tests.
🇪🇺
ECE 22.06
United Nations Economic Commission for Europe. Latest standard with enhanced rotational impact protection.
🏆
SNELL
Strictest voluntary helmet safety standard. Surpasses government requirements. For premium racing helmets.
🛡️
CE Level 2
European Conformity for riding gear. Level 2 is highest protection for jackets, gloves, and knee guards.
Our Journey

15 YEARS OF TRUST

2012
The Beginning 🌱
Sri Venkatachalapathy Agencies was founded by Navanithan KJ in Thanjavur with a vision to provide quality bike accessories to Tamil Nadu riders.
2015
Expanding Range 🚀
Introduced international branded helmets including AGV, LS2, and HJC. Became the go-to destination for premium helmets in Thanjavur.
2018
5000+ Customers 🎉
Crossed the milestone of 5000 happy customers. Started offering personalized helmet fitting services and safety consultations.
2020
Parcel Service Launch 📦
Launched pan-Tamil Nadu parcel delivery service. Riders from Coimbatore to Chennai could now order premium gear from our store.
2023
10,000+ Customers 🏆
Achieved the landmark of 10,000+ satisfied customers. Expanded riding jacket and adventure gear collection significantly.
2026
Still Going Strong 💪
15 years and still Thanjavur's most trusted bike accessories store. Now available online. Your safety is still our priority!
